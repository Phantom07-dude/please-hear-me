Subject: Formal request for approval of a post-incourse/Christmas staycation

Dear Princess,

First and foremost, Phantom wishes to acknowledge the completion of the Princess’s ongoing academic engagements and to congratulate her on reaching the end of her test period. He firmly believes that the examinations went well and that she is finally approaching a period in which academic responsibilities may be temporarily placed on administrative hold.

Following extensive internal deliberation, careful consideration of the events of the outgoing year, and a thorough assessment of recreational needs, Phantom has deemed it necessary to submit the following proposal for the Princess’s kind consideration.

Article I: Purpose of Application

This correspondence is a formal request for the Princess’s approval and participation in a proposed post-incourse/Christmas staycation, to be organised at a location already identified and secured by Phantom.

Phantom considers this arrangement a necessary intervention in the interest of relaxation, companionship, emotional replenishment and the general preservation of romantic relations.

Article II: Justification and Grounds for Approval

It is recognised that the outgoing year has been nothing short of peculiar. It has presented its fair share of unexpected developments, academic demands and other experiences.

It is therefore Phantom’s position that concluding such a year without a proper vacation would constitute a rather unfortunate administrative oversight.

The proposed staycation is intended to provide:

  1. A well-deserved opportunity to rest, recover and temporarily disengage from the demands of everyday life.
  2. An uninterrupted period of companionship, affection and quality time.
  3. An opportunity to create beautiful memories, engage in spontaneous activities and enjoy each other’s company without distractions.
  4. A much-needed change of environment and a temporary suspension of the seriousness of life.

Phantom further submits that there is no better way to conclude the year than by spending some meaningful time together with the Princess.

Article III: Proposed Activities and Recreational Programme

Subject to the Princess’s approval, the following activities are proposed for the duration of the staycation:

  • Adequate sleeping and resting arrangements, with no unnecessary disturbances.
  • Eating, and occasionally making questionable decisions about what to eat next.
  • Watching movies, watching reels, having conversations and generally enjoying each other’s presence.
  • Random laughter, videos, unnecessary teasing and other activities that cannot be adequately documented in an official report.
  • Evening and nighttime strolls, with provisions made for purchasing desserts on the way back.

Article IV: Special Provision, the Perfect Excuse

Phantom wishes to formally notify the Princess that an appropriate excuse has already been secured to facilitate her attendance.

This provision has been carefully considered and is expected to answer any reasonable enquiries that may arise concerning the proposed trip.

Further details shall be communicated through the appropriate private channels when necessary.

The Princess is therefore assured that this matter has not been approached without adequate preparation.

Article V: Terms, Conditions and General Understanding

For the avoidance of doubt, the following provisions are hereby established:

  1. This application is a request for consideration and not in any way an order.
  2. The proposed arrangements shall be subject to mutual agreement.
  3. No academic obligation, personal responsibility or legitimate commitment shall be disregarded in the process.
  4. The primary objective remains spending quality time together, making memories and enjoying a much-needed break.
  5. The Princess reserves the right to recommend an extension of the staycation should the circumstances prove particularly favourable.

Article VI: Final Appeal and Request for Approval

Having presented the foregoing justifications and supporting arguments, Phantom sincerely hopes that this application receives favourable consideration.

Phantom honestly believes that this would be a beautiful way to spend part of the Christmas season, reconnect, unwind and give themselves something genuinely lovely to remember about this year.

Beyond the formal language and administrative arrangements, the truth is simple:

I want to spend this time with you, please.

I want the random conversations, the laughter, the movies, the night talks, the little moments and all the memories that will come with having you around.

Phantom would therefore appreciate the Princess’s favourable response and, most importantly, her approval to proceed with the proposed arrangements.

Thank you for your anticipated cooperation and consideration. Thank you for your attention to this serious matter.

Yours, in all things!

Phantom
